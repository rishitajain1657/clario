The Architecture

How Clario Works: From Noise to Signal.

A deterministic, three-stage intelligence pipeline engineered to parse chaotic campus announcements without ever hallucinating facts or making decisions for the student.

STAGE 01 // INGESTION

Multimodal Stream Parser

Accepts forwarded WhatsApp chats, messy email circulars, handwritten notice photos, and syllabus PDFs.

  • OCR Vision processing for flyer posters
  • Structured entity extraction
  • Multi-format support (.pdf, .png, .txt)
STAGE 02 // TRIAGE

Collision & De-Duplication

Aggregates 10 forwards into 1 event. Cross-references your timetable in real time to detect conflicting commitments.

  • Automated clash detection
  • Source fingerprinting (No duplicates)
  • Incomplete notice flagging
STAGE 03 // ACTION

Five Priorities Delivery

Presents what matters, why it matters, and the exact next step without taking autonomy away from the student.

  • One-click Campus Dashboard sync
  • Urgency & consequence tags
  • Zero hallucinated details

Competitive Advantage

What Other Teams Miss.

Most hackathon solutions are simple chatbot wrappers that hallucinate dates and force rigid recommendations. Here is how Clario fundamentally solves the problem statement.

Challenge Dimension Typical Hackathon Team ✦ Clario Architecture
Handling Incomplete Notices Generic LLMs guess or hallucinate missing venues, timings, or prerequisites. Strict Grounding: Never invents details. Flags incomplete circulars with explicit "Missing Venue — Check with Organizer" tags.
Student Agency Makes rigid, prescriptive decisions ("You should attend X instead of Y"). Preserves Student Agency: Highlights urgency, clashes, and consequences so the student remains in full control.
Schedule Clashes Treats each announcement in isolation, completely missing timetable overlap. Collision Detection Engine: Automatically detects overlapping labs, orientations, and deadlines in real-time.
Duplicate WhatsApp Forwards Creates separate clutter cards for every forwarded message across 5 groups. Intelligent De-Duplication: Fuses duplicate forwards into a single verified master update with unified source tags.
Multimodal Real-World Inputs Text-only chatbots that fail when CRs send poster images or PDF timetables. Native Multimodal Vision: Drop real screenshots, posters, and syllabi; extracts deadlines in <12ms.
