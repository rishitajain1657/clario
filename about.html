Our Identity

CLARIO

Scroll less. Know more.

Born from the belief that technology should make life clearer, Clario answers one of a fresher’s most familiar problems: information overload.

College information is everywhere. Clarity is not.

Announcements arrive through WhatsApp groups, emails, notice boards, portals, forwards, and conversations. The information exists, but finding what actually matters can become the real challenge. Clario transforms that noise into a clear, personal next step.

The Name, Unfolded

CLAR + I + O

Every part of the name describes the experience Clario is built to create.

CLAR

Clarity

Inspired by clarus, a Latin root for clear, bright, distinct, and understandable. Clario makes campus information easier to see, understand, and act upon.

I

Intelligence

The intelligent layer that organizes and prioritizes information, moving beyond collecting announcements to showing what deserves attention.

O

Overview

One unified view of campus life, where a student can see what is happening, what matters, and what needs attention now.

CLAR + I + O = Clario Intelligence that turns information into clarity.

The Logo

A visual story of noise becoming clarity.

The logo maps the exact problem Clario solves. The cloud of dots at the top represents the 50+ scattered messages a student receives across groups, portals, emails, and forwards. Each dot is information competing for attention.

The twisted lines below represent the chaos of that stream: duplicate forwards, mixed priorities, missing details, and overlapping announcements. Clario reads through this confusion and filters what is actually relevant.

The single, thicker stem represents the result: one clear line of clarity. It connects directly to the Clario wordmark because every scattered message should lead to one understandable next action.

Many messages in. One clear next move out.
